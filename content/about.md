Arabesque for Zola is a theme for [Zola](https://www.getzola.org), the static site generator. Its look and its rules come from the [Arabesque design system](https://design.arabesquelabs.com): the tokens, the CSS and the small helpers, copied into the theme unchanged.

## Where the design comes from

Arabesque patterns look intricate, but they come from a few simple shapes and one rule, repeated with care. The system draws its pattern the same way, by rule, from the 4.8.8 tiling: octagons and squares, with strands leaving each edge's midpoint at a fixed angle.

{% <quote name="The design system's principles" role="Arabesque"> %}
The fewest steps to get something done, actions that say exactly what they do, and every consequence shown before it happens.
{% </quote> %}

## What it leaves out

- **No tracking, and nothing from other sites unless you embed it.** Fonts are served from your own site; a video, a gist or a diagram loads from elsewhere only on the page that has one.
- **No framework.** Plain HTML and CSS; a small script adds the pattern, copy buttons and the like.
