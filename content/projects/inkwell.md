Writing a paragraph that moves between Arabic and English is where most editors stumble: the cursor jumps, a full stop lands on the wrong side, a selection covers what you didn't mean. Inkwell sets each paragraph's direction from its first strong letter and keeps the cursor where your eye expects it.
