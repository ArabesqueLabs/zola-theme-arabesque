Lantern reads RSS and Atom feeds on your own server and shows them as one column, oldest first, with no unread counts to chase. It keeps a week of articles and forgets the rest.

## Why

Most readers turn feeds into an inbox. Lantern treats them as a newspaper: it comes once a day, and when you've read it, you're done.

## What's in it

- A single binary and a SQLite file.
- Full text fetched for feeds that only send a summary.
- Right-to-left feeds shown right to left.
